In an exploratory follow-up on the same 900 positions, we predicted held-out terminal scores using other configurations and disjoint repetitions. Mean squared prediction error was 0.1215–0.1216 at H ≥ 35, 0.0829 at H = 15–34, and 0.0254 below 15. After excluding unresolved positions and positions where every tested continuation won or every continuation lost, errors were 0.1226, 0.1129 and 0.0804 (n = 297, 220, 95). A within-game comparison of 42 such pairs also favored low H. These results support greater predictability under our fixed policies; they do not isolate card visibility from game progress.
